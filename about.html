Keeping Khmer food heritage alive

Meatophum began with a simple belief: the best Cambodian food is the food families eat at home — a bowl of prahok, a basket of freshly picked herbs, and rice shared around the table.

We specialise in prahok and kreoung anlok — the dips, pounded salads and sour soups Cambodians grow up with — alongside regional favourites like Battambang teuk kreoung and Siem Reap–style pounded chicken.

We keep prices honest so everyone can eat well: most dishes cost around $2.5, served in a clean, welcoming dining room at our two Phnom Penh branches.
