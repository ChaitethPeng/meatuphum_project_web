Frequently asked

Is prahok very strong for first-timers?+

Try the Creamy Prahok with Pork or Grilled Prahok Kreoung first — cooking mellows the flavour.

How much does a meal cost?+

Most dishes are around $2.5 (10,000–25,000៛). Desserts are 4,000៛.

Is there parking?+

Parking is limited, especially at lunchtime. Coming by tuk-tuk or outside peak hours is easiest.

Do you take group bookings?+

Yes — for 10+ guests please call or book a day ahead.
