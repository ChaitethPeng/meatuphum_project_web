The Khmer way

របៀបញ៉ាំគ្រឿងអន្លក់

Kreoung anlok is a shared-table ritual: one bold dip in the middle, a mountain of raw and blanched vegetables around it, and hot jasmine rice for everyone.

  1. ១

    Pick your dip

    Prahok, kapi or teuk kreoung — mild to fearless.

  2. ២

    Grab the greens

    Cucumber, long bean, eggplant, banana blossom, wild leaves.

  3. ៣

    Dip, scoop, repeat

    Balance every bite with rice and a pounded salad.
